## Description
Type a story in any language and get a short film: acting characters, neural voices, an original score. There's no image generator. A crew of NVIDIA Nemotron models on Nebius Token Factory writes every frame as code, and a deterministic engine renders and measures it.

- Ultra (Director) plans the shots, the coverage and a cast & set bible
- Super (Cast/Artist) designs the characters as engine kits and draws each frame as SVG
- The engine measures every render; a vision model looks and Nemotron Nano scores; Nano edits
- Series mode keeps a host pixel-identical across daily episodes
