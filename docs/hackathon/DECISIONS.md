| D17 | 2026-09-26 | **Render-measured quality gates** in `validateDrawing`/`validateLibrary`: (1) every character of the shot is visible, and the largest one's **visible height measured by pixel difference** meets the shot type's minimum (close-up 75 %, medium 45 %, wide 25 %, else 30 %); (2) night scenes (`NIGHT_WORDS`) render with mean brightness ≤ 120; (3) set symbols cover ≥ 97 % of the frame; (4) minimum detail (set 15 shapes, character 12, prop 5) and viewBox aspect per kind. | D31 | 2026-10-09 | **Characters act** (SPEC v2 WP4.1, `acting.ts` + kit poses). The Director gives every visible character a per-shot `acting` cue: pose ∈ stand, walk, sit, wave, point, hold, hug, bow, look-left, look-right, kneel; expression ∈ neutral, smile, laugh, sad, surprised, sleepy. After the Cast, the engine builds **only the variants the plan uses** from the same kit spec (same parts, colours and gradient), so identity is kept by construction. A test renders the head of stand vs wave/point/hold/hug and requires **identical pixels**. At commit, an acting layer of plain 2D construct shapes rides the camera: **blinks** (eyelid overlays shown for exactly 2 frames at seeded times; none when the expression already closes the eyes), **lip-sync** (a mouth overlay whose height follows the engine's measured envelope of the speaker's recorded line, `lipCurvesOf`), and **walks**.
